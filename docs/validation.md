## Investigation ablation

Source: `investigation-benchmark.json`, generated by `scripts/benchmark_investigation.py`. Every strategy uses the same initial hypotheses, fixed costs and required-record policy. All four conservative releases were verified.

| Policy | Confirmed | Rejected | Unresolved | Lower cost | Conservative cost | Retained | Method inspections |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| No investigation | 0 | 0 | 8 | 0 | 27 | 7 | 0 |
| Exact only | 3 | 0 | 5 | 6 | 27 | 7 | 8 |
| Exact, then AST | 4 | 0 | 4 | 7 | 27 | 7 | 13 |
| Full deterministic MCP policy | 5 | 1 | 2 | 10 | 22 | 8 | 17 |

The full policy makes eight candidate-level MCP investigation calls, which execute eight exact checks, five AST checks and four graph-provenance assessments. Rejecting the unsupported scaffold-based conflict lowers the conservative optimum by five units and retains one more record. Confirming true edges raises the lower bound; it does not by itself lower the conservative upper bound, since unresolved edges were already included there.

Simple training-only or evaluation-only blanket exclusions are infeasible under this fixture's required-record policy. They are reported as infeasible and are not presented as valid cost baselines.

This is a synthetic evidence-tool ablation. The fixture is constructed to exercise these mechanisms; the results do not estimate real-world detection accuracy, false-negative rate or live-model performance.

## Solver comparison

Source: `solver-benchmark.json`, five measured repetitions per size after warmup. Startup is excluded. Both paths read the same FalkorDB snapshot; the reference then builds and solves a NetworkX graph in memory. Native end-to-end time includes graph reads, temporary-network writes, native max-flow, certificate checking and exact temporary-graph cleanup.

| Records | Conflicts | Native end-to-end median | Native procedure median | NetworkX with graph snapshot median |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 40 | 70 | 19.642 ms | 3.592 ms | 3.709 ms |
| 200 | 397 | 132.449 ms | 14.911 ms | 27.198 ms |
| 800 | 1,599 | 980.249 ms | 27.190 ms | 151.808 ms |

All optimum costs agree. The current native end-to-end path is slower on these cases. Procedure-only time is a component measurement and must not be substituted for application latency. No SQL/PostgreSQL baseline was run, and no claim of FalkorDB speed superiority is supported.
